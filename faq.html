Frequently Asked Questions

What is AutoList Canada?

▼

AutoList Canada is an AI-powered cross-listing platform designed specifically for Canadian sellers. It helps you optimize your listings for different marketplaces and manage them from a single dashboard, saving you time and improving your sales strategy.

How is this different from other cross-listing tools?

▼

We focus on more than just automation. Our tools use behavioral science and market-specific logic to help you create listings that resonate with buyers. We prioritize your data privacy and offer a suite of AI tools that work even without connecting your marketplace accounts.

Which marketplaces do you support?

▼

We are constantly expanding our supported platforms. Currently, we support major Canadian marketplaces like Kijiji, Facebook Marketplace, and Poshmark Canada, with more integrations planned for the future. Check our Features page for the most up-to-date list.

Do I need an account to use the AI tools?

▼

While some basic tools may be accessible without an account, a free AutoList Canada account is required to save your work, access advanced features, and manage your listings. Signing up is quick and easy.

How do I reset my password?

▼

You can reset your password by clicking the "Forgot Password?" link on the login page. You will receive an email with instructions on how to create a new password for your account.

Is there a free trial?

▼

Yes, we offer a free plan that includes access to our core AI tools and a limited number of listings. For unlimited listings and advanced features, you can upgrade to one of our paid plans. See our Pricing page for details.

How can I cancel my subscription?

▼

You can manage your subscription, including cancellations, from your account's billing settings. If you cancel, you will retain access to your plan's features until the end of the current billing cycle.

Do you store my marketplace login credentials?

▼

No. We never ask for or store your marketplace login credentials. Account connections are handled through secure, official API integrations (OAuth) where available, which means we never see your password. Your security is our top priority.

Will AutoList post to my accounts automatically?

▼

Never. You are always in full control. AutoList Canada will only perform actions like posting or updating listings when you explicitly authorize it. We believe in empowering sellers, not taking over their accounts.
