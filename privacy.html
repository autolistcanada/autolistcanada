Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 19, 2024

1. Introduction

AutoList Canada (“we”, “us”, “our”) operates the autolist.ca website and related services. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our site.

2. What We Collect

  • Personal Information: Name, email, phone (if provided by you).
  • Device & Usage Data: IP address, browser type, device info, site usage analytics.
  • Listings Data: Information you submit about items, including photos and descriptions.
  • AI Interactions: Content you submit to our AI tools or chat features.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and improve our services.
  • To personalize your experience.
  • To communicate with you (if you opt in).
  • To monitor, secure, and analyze site usage.
  • To develop new features, including AI-powered tools.

4. Data Protection

We are committed to protecting your data. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest, we use secure cloud infrastructure, and access to your data is strictly controlled.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal information. For any privacy-related requests, please contact us at privacy@autolist.ca.

6. Contact Us

For questions or concerns about your privacy, you can reach our team at privacy@autolist.ca.
