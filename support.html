Need Support?

We’re here to help. Whether you’re stuck, scaling, or syncing—AutoList Canada has your back.

Reach our seller-first support team at support@autolist.ca. We respond in under 24 hours. Mark "URGENT" in the subject for priority issues.

Response Time

6–12 hours on average. Our team checks the inbox daily because sellers can't afford to wait. Weekend response times may vary slightly.

Sync & Listing Issues

Listings not updating? Try refreshing your sync settings or re-verifying marketplace tokens from the dashboard.

AI Output Concerns

If AI-generated content seems off, send us your prompt and listing details. We’ll analyze it with our fine-tuned models.
